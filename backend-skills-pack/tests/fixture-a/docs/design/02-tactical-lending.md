## Decisions
- D2-1 Three aggregates: BookCirculation (one per Lending Book), Borrower (one per member) and HoldReadyNotification (one per hold-shelf placement). Why: these are the three invariant clusters below. Proposed.
- D2-2 Copies, active loans and open holds of one book live in BookCirculation. Why: FIFO holds (REQ-7), no renewal while held (REQ-13), hold shelf on check in (REQ-17, REQ-21), no withdraw while on loan (REQ-27) and hold only when no copy is available (REQ-6, Q-2) all relate the copies, loans and holds of the same book in one transaction. Proposed.
- D2-3 The aggregate is named BookCirculation, not Book, so it is not confused with the Catalog Book aggregate (DOC-04, Ubiquitous language "Book" in Lending). Proposed.
- D2-4 The 3-loan limit (REQ-8) and the fine block (REQ-9) are per member, across books, so they live in Borrower as loan slots. Borrowing is a two-step process: BorrowBook reserves a slot in Borrower (LoanSlotReserved), then policy LendCopyOnSlotReserved runs LendCopy on BookCirculation, which emits LoanStarted or LoanRefused; LoanRefused releases the slot. Why: TAC-03, one aggregate per transaction; the slot is reserved first because it is the cheap side to compensate. Proposed; see LQ-1.
- D2-5 The loan id is generated by BorrowBook and is the slot id, so LoanEnded and LoanRefused release the right slot and LendCopy is idempotent on a redelivered LoanSlotReserved. Proposed.
- D2-6 BorrowBook pre-checks, by reading BookCirculation without changing it, that the book is known and a copy is available to this member, to fail fast. The final check is in LendCopy. Books are never removed (Q-9), so BookUnknown cannot appear later. Proposed.
- D2-7 Barcode uniqueness across all books (Q-10) spans BookCirculation instances, so it is a store-enforced unique index, not an aggregate invariant. AddCopy fails with BarcodeTaken on violation. Proposed.
- D2-8 The 3-day hold-shelf timer (REQ-17) uses delayed delivery of CopyPlacedOnHoldShelf: the outbox row carries "deliver not before = pickupDeadline", and policy ExpireHoldOnShelfDeadline then runs ExpireShelvedHold, which is a no-op if the hold was picked up or cancelled. Proposed; needs a not-before column in the Lending outbox (phase 3).
- D2-9 BookCirculation loads only active loans and open holds (Waiting, OnShelf). Ended loans and closed holds stay as history rows read by queries, never loaded into the aggregate. Why: keeps the aggregate small (TAC-11). Proposed.
- D2-10 Lending receives the member id from the signed-in member resolved by Membership (ResolveCurrentMember, REQ-29, D1-7) at the API boundary and trusts it. Proposed.
- D2-11 Borrower keeps a local copy of the fine status from FineBalanceChanged (D1-5); RecordFineStatus applies an event only when its accountVersion (sent by Fines, 03 DQ-3) is greater than the stored one, so out-of-order delivery, including two events in the same millisecond, cannot unblock a member. asOf (event occurredAt) is kept for display only and never used for ordering. Proposed.
- D2-12 BookAvailabilityChanged carries a full snapshot (available, total, earliest due date) on every change of any of them, so Catalog can upsert without ordering logic beyond the version. Counts follow SQ-2 (withdrawn excluded from total) and SQ-3 (hold-shelf copies not available). Proposed.
- D2-13 Defaults adopted from 01 until answered: Q-2 (no hold when a copy is available), Q-3 (limit counts loans only), Q-4 (expired hold passes the copy on), Q-6 (any balance blocks), Q-11 (calendar days), Q-12 (reject check in of a copy not on loan), SQ-5 (email failure does not stop the hold shelf). Proposed.
- D2-14 Email goes through an outbound EmailPort with an anticorruption adapter (D1-8). HoldReadyNotification records each send so retries are idempotent (keyed by the CopyPlacedOnHoldShelf event id). Proposed.
- D2-15 A refused borrow is kept, not deleted: CancelLoanSlot marks the LoanSlot Refused, with refusedAt and reason. A Refused slot no longer counts toward the 3-slot limit or the one-slot-per-book rule, and like ended loans (D2-9) it leaves the loaded aggregate and stays as a history row. GetBorrowRequest reads it so a member who got 202 can see the outcome (06 AQ-1). Retention is LQ-12. Proposed.
- Fixed F4: PlaceHold `Fails when` now lists BorrowerUnknown (MemberRegistered not yet applied in Lending, so the hold cannot reference a Borrower).
- Fixed F5: D2-11, the FineStatus row and RecordFineStatus now order by accountVersion; asOf is kept for display only. This closes DQ-3 (03-db-design.md) with its proposed default, matching `fine_status_account_version` in 03.
- Fixed F6: added query GetBorrowRequest over the kept Refused LoanSlot record (D2-15), as 06 AQ-1 proposes; LoanSlot gains state, refusedAt and reason; CancelLoanSlot keeps the record instead of removing it.
- Fixed TAC-07: REQ-30 (pick up a shelved hold copy) is mapped to the existing BorrowBook then LendCopy path, the "or on the shelf for this member" case (LendCopy lends the OnShelf copy to its holder and sets the hold Fulfilled; LoanStarted carries fromHoldId). This is the proposed default of RQ-1, pending answer; RQ-1 and LQ-13 stay open. REQ-30 added to the REQ cells of BorrowBook and LendCopy and to Inputs.
- Lint L4 (TAC-04) on CopyPlacedOnHoldShelf: rejected by the reviewer; the name is a past-tense fact (`<Noun><PastVerb>` plus a place suffix), so it is kept unchanged.

## Open questions
| Id | Question | Why it matters | Proposed default |
|---|---|---|---|
| LQ-1 | Is it acceptable that Borrow answers "accepted" and the loan appears within seconds (D2-4), with a rare refusal afterwards if the last copy was just taken? | Member UX on S2; API status code in phase 6. | Yes; BorrowBook returns 202 with the loan id; the handler waits briefly for the in-process outcome and returns 201 when it arrives in time. |
| LQ-2 | Can an overdue loan be renewed? (Q-8) | RenewLoan failure LoanOverdue. | Not decided by the brief; proposed default: allowed. |
| LQ-3 | Does a renewal add 14 days to the current due date or to the renewal day? (REQ-12) | Due date after RenewLoan. | Current due date plus 14 days. |
| LQ-4 | When a copy is added (REQ-25) while holds are waiting, does it go to the hold shelf for the first hold? | AddCopy outcome; fairness of REQ-7. | Yes, same as check in (REQ-21). |
| LQ-5 | Can a copy on the hold shelf be withdrawn (REQ-26), and what happens to its hold? | Only "on loan" blocks withdraw (REQ-27). | Allowed; the hold goes back to Waiting at its original queue place and the next copy serves it. |
| LQ-6 | Can a member place two open holds on the same book, hold a book they have on loan, or borrow a second copy of a book they have on loan? | Invariants of Borrower and BookCirculation. | Reject all three (HoldAlreadyPlaced, BookOnLoanToMember, LoanForBookExists). |
| LQ-7 | Do owed fines block placing a hold, or only borrowing? (REQ-9) | PlaceHold failures. | Only borrowing, as REQ-9 says; picking up from the shelf is a borrow and is blocked. |
| LQ-8 | Is there a maximum number of open holds per member? | PlaceHold failures. | No limit. |
| LQ-9 | Is the barcode unique across the whole library? (Q-10) | D2-7 unique index. | Yes. |
| LQ-10 | May a librarian cancel a member's hold, or check in for a member who lost the copy? | Who may act on CancelHold and CheckInCopy. | No; only the holding member cancels (REQ-15); lost copies are withdrawn only after check in. |
| LQ-11 | What does Days late on the overdue list (REQ-22) count? (Q-11) | ListOverdueLoans output. | Calendar days between due date and today, in the library's time zone. |
| LQ-12 | How long does a member see a refused borrow (GetBorrowRequest, D2-15)? | Size of kept Refused slot rows; how long a replayed borrow key can show the refusal. | Keep Refused slots at least as long as idempotency keys (24 hours, 06 D6-8); purge after 30 days. |
| RQ-4 | (07 review) Is the 3-day hold-shelf period 72 hours from check in, or the end of the third calendar day? | Sets pickupDeadline for ExpireShelvedHold (REQ-17). | End of the third calendar day in the library time zone. |
| LQ-13 | How does the holder take a copy waiting on the hold shelf? (07 RQ-1, F9) | LendCopy already lends a shelf copy to its holder through BorrowBook; REQ-5 hides Borrow when no copy is available. | The holder uses Borrow, as LendCopy assumes; no design change until answered. |

## Aggregates
| Aggregate | Root entity | Contains (entities, value objects) | References by id | Invariants (REQ) | Created by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BookCirculation | BookCirculation | Copy, Loan, Hold, Barcode, BibliographicSummary, PickupDeadline | Book (Catalog) by bookId, Borrower by memberId | (a) A copy is in exactly one state: Available, OnLoan, OnHoldShelf, Withdrawn (01 Copy). (b) A copy has at most one active loan (proposed). (c) A loan's due date is the borrowed date plus 14 days (REQ-10). (d) A loan is renewed at most once, for another 14 days (REQ-12, LQ-3). (e) No renewal while a Waiting hold exists for the book (REQ-13). (f) A hold may be placed only when no copy is Available (REQ-6, Q-2, SQ-3). (g) Holds are served in order of placement (REQ-7). (h) A returned or added copy goes to the hold shelf for the first Waiting hold, if any (REQ-21, LQ-4). (i) A copy on the hold shelf can be lent only to its holder, until its pickup deadline of 3 days (REQ-17). (j) A copy on loan cannot be withdrawn (REQ-27). (k) Check in only for a copy on loan (REQ-20, Q-12). (l) At most one open hold per member (LQ-6). Barcode uniqueness across books is store-enforced, not here (D2-7). | policy:OpenCirculationOnBookAdded |
| Borrower | Borrower | LoanSlot, ContactDetails, FineStatus | Member (Membership) by memberId, BookCirculation by bookId | (a) At most 3 loan slots, reserved or active (REQ-8, Q-3). (b) No slot may be reserved while FineStatus says a balance is owed (REQ-9, Q-6); this is eventually consistent with Fines through FineBalanceChanged (D1-5, SQ-1). (c) At most one slot per book (LQ-6). Refused slots count toward neither (D2-15). Slots are eventually consistent with BookCirculation loans: released by policy after LoanRefused or LoanEnded (D2-4). | policy:RegisterBorrowerOnMemberRegistered |
| HoldReadyNotification | HoldReadyNotification | ContactDetails, PickupDeadline | BookCirculation by bookId, Borrower by memberId, Hold by holdId | (a) One email per hold-shelf placement; a sent notification is never sent again (REQ-16, D2-14). | policy:NotifyHolderOnHoldShelf |

## Policies
| Policy | When (event) | Then (command) | Context | On failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RegisterBorrowerOnMemberRegistered | MemberRegistered | RegisterBorrower | Membership to Lending | Retry with backoff through the inbox; alert after repeated failure. Idempotent by memberId. |
| UpdateFineBlockOnBalanceChanged | FineBalanceChanged | RecordFineStatus | Fines to Lending | BorrowerUnknown: retry until MemberRegistered is applied, then alert. Older events are ignored (D2-11). |
| OpenCirculationOnBookAdded | BookAdded | OpenBookCirculation | Catalog to Lending | Retry with backoff; alert after repeated failure. Idempotent by bookId. |
| LendCopyOnSlotReserved | LoanSlotReserved | LendCopy | Lending | Technical failure (version conflict, outage): retry. Business refusal is the LoanRefused event, not a failure. |
| CancelSlotOnLoanRefused | LoanRefused | CancelLoanSlot | Lending | Retry; alert after repeated failure (a stuck slot blocks one of the member's 3 loans). |
| ReleaseSlotOnLoanEnded | LoanEnded | ReleaseLoanSlot | Lending | Retry; alert after repeated failure. |
| NotifyHolderOnHoldShelf | CopyPlacedOnHoldShelf | SendHoldReadyEmail | Lending | Retry with backoff; alert after repeated failure. The hold stays on the shelf either way (SQ-5). |
| ExpireHoldOnShelfDeadline | CopyPlacedOnHoldShelf | ExpireShelvedHold | Lending | Version conflict: retry. No-op when already picked up or cancelled. |
